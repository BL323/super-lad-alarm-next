Story

Our hero "Naylor" is on an adventure known as "The Lad Alarm Train Crawl".

Throughout the day he travels by train along the Dorset line, from pub to pub. Each leg of the crawl is one level: he leaves the last pub, catches a train, hops off at the next station and walks through the local village to the next pub.

The crawl starts at Poole, runs west to Dorchester, then comes back east along the line to finish at Parkstone.

Legs, stations and pubs.

1. Poole → Dorchester South, Brewhouse
2. Dorchester South → Moreton, Frampton
3. Moreton → Wool, The Black Bear (angry locals)
4. Wool → Wareham, The Old Granary (an art easel ends up in the river; the Kings Arms is in the background)
5. Wareham → Poole, The Rising Sun
6. Poole → Parkstone, The Avocet
7. Inside The Avocet, to the bar: the landlord isn't serving (the last boss)
8. The Avocet → Parkstone Kebabs, before it shuts (with a megaphone left on the pub step)

Naylor's arch nemesis, "The Train Conductor", stands in his way twice. He first fights Naylor on the roof of his train between Moreton and Wool. He comes back for a harder rematch between Poole and Parkstone. Inside The Avocet the landlord won't serve Naylor his last pint: he's beaten with the pub itself (the beer pumps, his own barrels, the last orders bell and the cellar hatch). Then it's out through Parkstone's streets at closing time, past foxes, bins, traffic and bouncers, to the kebab shop.

Location: Southern England, UK. The trains should look like British trains and the pubs like traditional pubs. Wareham is by a river, and Poole by the harbour. The day goes from late morning to evening as the crawl goes on.

The pubs can appear in the background as buildings. Naylor hops along train-like objects as the platforms.

Our hero collects 'pints': glasses of a stout-style drink, black body with a white top.

There is also a bonus espresso martini hidden in each level.
